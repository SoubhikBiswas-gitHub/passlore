# Code of conduct

Everyone contributing to Passlore should be able to participate respectfully.

- Be welcoming and considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique the work constructively and explain your reasoning.
- Respect privacy, consent and attribution.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate others or publish private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

The repository owner may edit or remove abusive content, restrict participation or block accounts. Use GitHub's “Report content” controls for harassment or abuse. Do not post private reports or personal details in a public issue.
